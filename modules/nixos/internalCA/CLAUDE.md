# internalCA

Trusts the **tactilecactus** internal CA — root cert at `root_ca.crt`. Wires the step user-CA into sshd and installs a systemd timer that renews the SSH host cert hourly via `pkgs.t11s.t11s-step` (a `step-cli` wrapper preconfigured with the CA bundle in `packages/t11s-step/step/`). Renewal only works on a cert that has not expired. `sudo t11s-sign-host-key` (installed on every sshd host) does the first signing, and re-signs an expired cert, using the `burke@t11s.net` provisioner (it prompts for the password). The cert key ID is `<host>.t11s.net` and its principals are `<host>.lan` and `<host>.dab-ling.ts.net`.

If the upstream CA bundle changes, the bundle in `packages/t11s-step/step/` needs updating alongside `root_ca.crt`.

`dot1x.enable` adds an 802.1x (EAP-TLS) client cert for WiFi/wired dot1x against turing's RADIUS. Cert + key live in `/var/lib/t11s-8021x/client.{crt,key}`, `root:wpa_supplicant` 0640 in a 0750 dir (NixOS runs wpa_supplicant unprivileged and chrooted, and it is what opens them; the module bind-mounts the dir read-only into its chroot), the root CA at `/etc/t11s-8021x/root_ca.crt` for NetworkManager's `802-1x.ca-cert`. `sudo t11s-sign-8021x` does the first issuance (and re-issues an expired cert) via the `devices` provisioner, which prompts for its password; `dot1x.class` (`personal`/`work`) becomes the cert OU that RADIUS maps to VLAN 100/300. The daily `t11s-8021x-renew` timer renews once the cert is within 30 days of expiry, and skips until the cert exists.
